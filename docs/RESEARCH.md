### Math, kindergarten – 2nd grade: NEW curriculum (read 2026-10-29)

⚠️ The 2006 grade documents in the table above are **superseded for grades 1–3**: the new curriculum applies to
grade 1 from 2024‑25, grade 2 from 2025‑26 and grade 3 from 2026‑27 (2025 letter p.2; 2026 letter p.2). Use these:
grade 1 `https://meyda.education.gov.il/files/Mazkirut_Pedagogit/math/primary-school/math2023/Newprogramgrade1.pdf`,
grade 2 `https://meyda.education.gov.il/files/Mazkirut_Pedagogit/math/primary-school/math2023/Newprogramgrade2.pdf`,
kindergarten (2010) `https://meyda.education.gov.il/files/Mazkirut_Pedagogit/Matematika/TochnitKdamYesodiHeb.pdf`.

- **Gan (pp.12–14):** count aloud 1–30 and back from 10; count objects to 20; compare groups by matching and counting;
  ordinals to 6th; estimate; symbols 0–10 (ext. 20), zero after the others; join/separate within 10 with objects;
  equal sharing to 10; "half" only as everyday language; dot dice; patterns, shapes, informal measuring, coins.
- **Grade 1:** numbers to 100 and 0 (~45h), operations within 100 (~45h), shapes, length, time (whole hours), data.
  Count forward/back to 100, by 10s/5s/2s; subitizing; grouping into 10s; read/write numbers incl. digit direction;
  number line (exact and approximate placement, misplaced number); between/before/after/closer to.
  Add/subtract within 10 then 20, any strategy; **firm command of all splits of 10**; = as "same amount"; missing
  numbers on either side; whole tens to 100 by analogy; money to 20 ₪ with change.
  **Not in grade 1:** ×/÷, odd/even, < > signs (all moved later).
- **Grade 2:** numbers to 1,000, place value and flexible splits, < >; counting by 10/20/50/100; odd/even (0 is even);
  **full command of facts within 20**; two-digit add/subtract in a row then columns; estimating, error finding;
  comparison word problems; multiplication as repeated addition/arrays — **2, 4, 5, 10 tables + division** (sharing
  and grouping); cm/m, perimeter; solids; clock to the half hour. **Not in grade 2:** parentheses, divisibility,
  fractions ½ ¼, area, weight.
- **Representations named:** number line, hundred chart, abacus, place-value "house", bundled sticks, coins, arrays,
  dot dice, digital manipulatives. Ten-frame and base-ten blocks are *not* named. Any reasonable strategy is accepted.
- **Item formats:** "who am I?" clues; missing numbers on a line or chart; misplaced number; missing addend on either
  side; build an exercise for a target; compare without solving; odd one out; open problems with several answers; money change.
- **Fluency** is framed as command/automatic recall, never speed — consistent with our no-timer rule.
- **Follow-up for the clock island:** the new grade 2 teaches the clock to the *half* hour; our `clock.quarter` skill is
  tagged grade 2 from the old curriculum — re-check its grade against the new grade 3 document.

### Hebrew (1st grade guidance, 2025–26)

- **Vowel order is not fixed nationally** ("varies between reading methods"). Ministry practice boards group:
  kamatz/patach/chataf-patach/shva → chirik → cholam → shuruk/kubutz → segol/tsere. Default to this; make it configurable.
- Required: all letters and vowel marks; אהו"י letters; same-sound letters ט/ת, ח/כ, ק/כ, ב/ו, ש/ס, א/ה/ע;
  dagesh pairs בּ/ב, כּ/כ, פּ/פ; final letters; writing each letter with correct stroke direction (rounded script).
- Phonological awareness: opening sound (צליל פותח), closing sound (צליל סוגר), segmenting and blending; rhyme and syllables in gan.
- Decoding: letter+vowel units blended into words (פִּי־צָה), then larger chunks by the end of 1st grade.
- Nikud is formally removed during 3rd grade.
- A new language curriculum for gan to 2nd grade is being published this school year — re-check before Stages 2–3.

### Hebrew letters and phonological awareness, gan to 1st grade (read 2026-10-29)

Sources: **[C]** curriculum gan–2nd (תשפ"ו) `…/Yesodi/tohnit-limudim-safa.pdf` · **[G]** required goals by age
`https://meyda.education.gov.il/files/Pop/0files/ivrit_hinuch_leshoni/Yesodi/acquisition-reading-writing.pdf` ·
**[H]** 1st-grade guidance `…/Yesodi/horaa.pdf` · **[R]** RAMA 1st-grade reading test, teacher guide
`https://meyda.education.gov.il/files/Rama/Yesodot_heb_1_teacher_instructions.pdf` · **[K]** gan letter-knowledge booklet
`https://meyda.education.gov.il/files/PreSchool/alef-bet1.pdf`

- **Ages 3–5** ([G] p.7, 10–11): rhyme; split words into syllables and blend them; opening syllable.
- **Ages 5–6, end of gan** ([G] p.16–19): segment/blend below the syllable (consonant+vowel units like בָּ);
  isolate the opening consonant+vowel; isolate the **closing consonant** (what ends דבש?); opening consonant tied to
  the letter's name and shape; syllable deletion (כפתור without כף → תור). Letter names matched to shapes.
  No required letter count; children arrive in 1st grade knowing ~18 of 22 on average ([C] p.77).
- **Teaching order hints:** full phoneme segmentation is "unnatural" in Hebrew; consonant+vowel is the accessible
  unit ([C] p.72). Start opening-sound work with continuous sounds ש ז ס ל ר מ נ ח ([G] p.18).
- **Names vs sounds:** teach both; a letter's name begins with its sound; activities may use either ([C] p.74, [G] p.18).
- **1st grade** ([G] p.26–31, [H]): no fixed letter order (depends on the school's reading method); all letters,
  finals during the year, same-sound letters ט/ת כ/ח ס/ש ב/ו א/ע/ה כ/ק, and בּ/ב כּ/כ פּ/פ שׁ/שׂ (sort words by
  hard/soft sound, right/left shin). Explicit teaching done within about six months.
- **Handwriting:** both print and cursive are taught in 1st grade, with emphasis on cursive (כתב עגול); letters grouped
  by stroke direction (arcs: ר ה ת ח ק ב; top-down: י ו מ נ ן). **No official stroke-order chart was found** — it lives in textbooks.
- **Digital use** ([C] p.157–160): simple, short, focused; large fonts, wide letter spacing, immediate feedback, no distracting effects.
- **RAMA assessment items to mirror** ([R]): letter names (22 cards; name or sound accepted; common confusions
  כ/ח, ו/ז, ק/כ, ת/ט); opening consonant — hear a sound, pick 1 of 4 pictures; closing consonant — see a picture,
  say the last sound; opening consonant+vowel — hear "סִי", pick סירה among 4 pictures.

### Nikud and reading, 1st–2nd grade (read 2026-10-29)

Sources as above, plus **[F]** ministry reading-fluency test for grades 2–6 (2021)
`https://meyda.education.gov.il/files/Mazkirut_Pedagogit/HebrewPrimary/assessment/fluency-assessment-2021.pdf`.

- **Nikud order is explicitly not binding** ("סדר סימני הניקוד אינו מחייב", [G] p.29); most methods start with /a/.
  Practice each sign alone (בָּנָה), then mixed (סִפֵּר). Units are taught as letter+sign (שִׁ) and letter+vowel letter (שִׁי) ([G] p.27).
  Drills: unit lists where only the letter or only the sign changes; matching a word-opening unit to pictures.
- **Vowel letters:** אהו"י as consonants and vowels (ו in ורד vs שוק); final /a/ /e/ written as sign + ה (שָׁנָה);
  contrast פר/פרה ([G] p.27–28). No grade 1–2 guidance on shva na/nach was found.
- **Decoding path (grade 1):** unit → word (פִּי/צָה → פיצה); words built from units; linear blending first
  (מִ-שְׁ-קֶ-פֶ-ת), larger chunks by end of grade 1. Always check meaning, "not reading for the sake of practice" ([G] p.28–29, [H] p.7, 32).
- **End of grade 1:** all letters, all nikud signs, אהו"י, homophones and homographs ([G] p.26).
- **Grade 2:** more automatic word recognition; prefixes (ו ה ש ב כ ל מ) and endings (ים/ות) read as units; a few
  frequent words without nikud; nikud removed formally only in grade 3 ([G] p.34–35, [H] p.5).
- **Fluency test [F]:** 80 pointed words, score = correct in 45 s; story read aloud with words-per-minute. No national norms.
  *Not used:* timed tasks are excluded for grades ≤ 2 by CLAUDE.md rules.
- **Item formats to mirror ([G], [R]):** match words and sentences to pictures; cloze in sentences (incl. homophones על/אל);
  semantic sorting / odd-one-out; build sentences from cards; order sentences into a story; missing unit beside a picture.
- **Shared and digital reading:** read-aloud and dialogic shared reading are "especially important in grade 1" ([H] p.15);
  books with prominent words in the signs already learned ([G] p.29). Digital: simple closed interface, large font,
  wide spacing; narration with word highlighting and adjustable speed are named as useful; extras only when tied to the plot ([C] p.157–160).

### English (Curriculum 2020, elementary)

- Mandatory from 3rd grade. Pre-A1 (Pre-Foundation) in 3rd and early 4th: listening and speaking first,
  then alphabet knowledge and phonics, reading built sound → letter → fluency.
- A1: Band I Core I (4th to mid-5th), Core II (mid-5th to end of 6th). About 1,200 words by end of 6th.
- Word lists: the ministry's "Lexical Bands" Excel file, linked from the English elementary page. Use it as the word database.
- 10+ encounters are needed to master a word → spaced review (DECISIONS D7).
Sources: `https://meyda.education.gov.il/files/Mazkirut_Pedagogit/English/curriculum2020Elementary.pdf`;
`https://pop.education.gov.il/learning-plans/english/elementary/`

## Best practices (research-backed)

- Hirsh-Pasek et al. 2015 ("four pillars"): active (thinking, not just tapping), engaged without distracting
  extras, meaningful (real-life context), socially interactive. `https://www.psychologicalscience.org/publications/educational-apps.html`
- Mastery-based progression; spaced repetition (Leitner); immediate informative feedback; adaptive help;
  short sessions for young children; spoken instructions with replay for pre-readers; multi-sensory (trace + hear + say).

## Technical notes

- **Hebrew TTS:** iOS has a he-IL voice; Android Chrome may list he-IL yet fall back to English; desktop Chrome
  often has none (Windows has "Asaf"). Isolated letter sounds and syllables are unreliable → record them (DECISIONS D3).
  Offline neural options for pre-generating: Phonikud, HebTTS.
- **Letter tracing:** no open Hebrew stroke-order dataset exists; author SVG stroke paths for 27 forms and check
  direction plus rough path with generous tolerance. English printable tracing SVGs: `github.com/domsson/tracedletters`.
- **Fonts (OFL):** Rubik (UI, bundled via `@fontsource-variable/rubik`); Noto Sans Hebrew or Alef for text with nikud
  (most reliable mark placement — verify visually); Playpen Sans Hebrew for handwriting practice.
- **Images:** OpenMoji (CC BY-SA 4.0), Twemoji (CC BY 4.0), Noto Emoji (Apache 2.0). Record any use in `docs/CREDITS.md`.
